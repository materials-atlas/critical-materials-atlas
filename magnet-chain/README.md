# Rare-earth magnet supply study

Evidence audit and reviewer handoff · 24 November 2026

## Publication assessment

The revised article can serve as a descriptive research briefing after editorial and specialist review. Its contribution is a same-month EU-27 import benchmark and scale test for an identified expansion. The Atlas’s earlier filed export-controls study already used the same product code in an event analysis against ferrite. The IEA already documents the concentration of production and the global diversification constraints at metallisation and magnet manufacturing. A publication claiming to identify the binding EU automotive bottleneck needs product-grade demand and qualified supply allocations by route. [IEA, Rare Earth Elements](https://www.iea.org/reports/rare-earth-elements/executive-summary).

From April to September 2025, recorded EU import mass from China under CN 85051110 fell 7.7% from the same months in 2024. Imports from other origins fell 38.7%. China-origin mass was higher in September than a year earlier and rose 7.2% for the full year. That illustrates why annual shares and a single event month can give different pictures. The larger decline in other-origin mass is a check against interpreting the year-on-year difference as an identified effect of the licensing notice. [Eurostat Comext DS-045409](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/ds-045409/default/table?lang=en) · [April 2025 notice](https://english.mofcom.gov.cn/Policies/AnnouncementsOrders/art/2025/art_0dd87cbee7b045bf93fabe6ab2faceee.html).

## Data and methods

- `out/comext_audit.json` saves the exact product definition, annual and monthly cells, status flags, units and queries. The dataset update is pinned in the snapshot. Convert 100 kg to tonnes by dividing by ten, then sum April through September in both years. Calculate the other-origin remainder from the official extra-EU total less China. For origin HHI, use the China-share contribution as a lower bound and allocate all remaining mass to one origin for the upper bound.
- `out/magnet_chain.json` collects stage estimates, selected facility observations, trade calculations and the six-month scenarios. Unknown available qualified tonnes are stored as null. Check the frozen BACI and historical pilot files for the respective archived extracts.
- The screen stops all China-origin direct imports in the April–September 2024 reference and retains the 1,097.579 tonnes recorded from other origins. The gross gap is 9,047.392 tonnes. For Phase 1B, an announced addition of 3,000 tonnes of annual blocks with one-third allocated to offshore wind leaves at most 1,000 tonnes for other uses in six months when both capacity and the wind allocation are prorated. If all wind deliveries fell outside the window, the nameplate-only six-month ceiling would be 1,500 tonnes before other constraints. Multiplying by a joint effective availability factor gives an illustrative credit of 0, 500 or 1,000 tonnes. Even the full ceiling leaves 8,047.392 tonnes of the import benchmark unfilled before other responses. This compares block capacity with customs mass under explicit one-for-one conversion and allocation assumptions. [Project disclosure](https://www.neomaterials.com/neos-european-integrated-magnet-project-selected-for-e126-million-grant-under-eu-innovation-fund/) · [November 2026 capacity basis](https://www.neomaterials.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/NPM-Q3-2026-Earnings-Transcript.pdf).
- A qualified route can be tested by converting the available output of each stage to finished units for one grade, with element-specific recipes and yields. Its primary output is bounded by the smallest stage limit. Compatible recycled alloy can add input at the alloy-to-magnet step, subject to available pressing, sintering and finishing slots. Stock and imported finished parts enter at delivery. Recalculate output with each limit relaxed and with jointly binding limits relaxed together. Compare interventions over the same demand, horizon and qualification timetable.

## Reviewer memo

**Scope and novelty.** A direct import flow is a defensible observable. Wholesale assignment of a mixed customs basket to automotive NdFeB would invalidate a claim about vehicle supply. A country-of-origin share at the magnet border cannot establish NdPr, Dy/Tb or alloy origin. The case-study amendment flags the unidentified 84% denominator and the two older verdict figures.

**Supply arithmetic.** The planned block expansion is a generous ceiling. Effective capacity can be zero if a grade is unavailable, feedstocks are constrained, or production is already allocated. Six months is a disruption window, not a project construction forecast. The disclosed package cost and timetable are bundled with separation and metallisation and cannot support a stand-alone cost-effectiveness ranking. The historical event was a licensing regime for selected products. The complete stop in the model tests the gross direct-import scale.

**Historical validation.** The article compares matched calendar months and checks observed recovery and other-origin flows. The prior filed Atlas test ruled out its prespecified 30% fall relative to ferrite in its early event window. It used a narrower partner filter that excluded Great Britain and Northern Ireland. The two results use different windows, comparators and import coverage. Keep the earlier result intact. [Atlas export-controls study](https://criticalmaterialsatlas.org/export-controls). Seek date-stamped customer deliveries, stock drawdowns and license or receipt dates to distinguish border timing, grade-specific shortages, anticipation and changes in demand. Compare the model against actual qualified orders filled and documented shutdowns, using a predeclared validation window and an import-share-only benchmark.

**Next evidence.** Select a defined set of EU traction-motor platforms and request six-month grade-specific demand, qualified suppliers, forward allocations, stock usable by customer, conversion yield at every stage and lead times for qualification. Native units should distinguish REO, contained Nd/Pr/Dy/Tb, alloy, blocks, finished parts and embedded magnets. Add sources from regulators and buyers alongside operator disclosures. For a simultaneous disruption to Chinese intermediates and finished magnets, trace upstream dependency for non-China suppliers before granting those routes full credit. Test material balances, supply monotonicity and alternative stock and demand ranges. Have a magnet-process specialist and a trade-data reviewer verify the resulting estimates before claiming a specific binding stage.

## Import-exposure addendum · 24 November 2026

The briefing quantifies direct-import exposure and the nominal mass comparison
with future Phase 1B blocks. The 2025 episode is historical context and a
plausibility check for shipment timing and scale. The observed product basket
cannot identify effective automotive capacity, unfilled end-use demand or a
causal effect of licensing.

The added `out/comext_origins.json` pins Comext annual observations for each
reported non-EU origin, including territories, and separately identifies
entries with no named origin. The fetcher saves reported net mass as integer
kilograms and retains observation flags. It checks that named and unnamed
rows reconcile exactly to the official extra-EU aggregate. The builder also
cross-checks the total and China cell against the original dated audit.

For named masses `m_i`, sum the squares of `m_i / sum(m_i)` for the named-origin
HHI. For the all-import range use the official extra-EU total `T` instead.
The lower limit is `sum(m_i²) / T²` and allows the unnamed mass `U` to be
dispersed. The upper limit assigns it all to the largest named origin and
adds `(2 × max(m_i) × U + U²) / T²` to the lower limit. These refinements leave
the older China-share-only bounds in place.

`python magnet-chain/record_magnet.py`. To refresh the detailed origins,
run `python magnet-chain/fetch_comext_origins.py`. If Eurostat revises the
annual China or extra-EU mass, refresh and reconcile both snapshots before
updating the dated figures.
