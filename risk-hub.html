Supply risk: where the methods agree

Headline figures on this page: modelled

The atlas scores supply risk five ways. They weigh concentration, governance, tonnes and the ability of supply to respond differently, so their rankings differ. A material that ranks high under all of them is a safer conclusion than one that tops a single list.

Ranked under each method

32 materials carry all five scores. Shaded cells are a top-10 rank. No material is in the top 10 under all 5. Gallium, Beryllium, Natural graphite, Rare-earth permanent magnets, Silicon, Magnesium, Phosphorus are in the top 10 under 4 of the 5. Across the ten pairs of methods, the median rank correlation is 0.53: the methods agree only in part.
