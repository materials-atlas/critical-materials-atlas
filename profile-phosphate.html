The origin gap. Phosphate rock is exported mainly by 🇯🇴 Jordan (24% of world trade) but mined mainly in 🇨🇳 China (44%), while Jordan mines almost none of it. Taken at face value, import-origin statistics point to Jordan as the source; one layer upstream, the real dependence is on China. Gap: +24 points.
