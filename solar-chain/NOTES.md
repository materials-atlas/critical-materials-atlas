# Solar study notes

The study follows the crystalline-silicon manufacturing sequence from
polysilicon through ingots, wafers, cells and modules. The inverter
comparison concerns deployment equipment. The [claim register](PRODUCTION.md)
gives the scope and publication date of each source.

- Historical production shares measure factory output. The cited figures
  mix technologies at the polysilicon and module ends as documented.
- Capacity reports give rated manufacturing capability at a point in time.
  The two IEA 2024 wafer figures come from different publications. The
  July 2026 commentary adds a separate 2025 context. These figures do not
  establish annual changes on a common basis.
- Europe in the IEA capacity estimates and the EU-27 in the Fraunhofer
  status inventory use different regional labels. The European ingot
  capacity in the IEA comparison remains missing.
- Fraunhofer’s historical silicon numbers are grams of polysilicon per
  watt-peak of crystalline-silicon cell output. Changes in cell output,
  wafer thickness, sawing loss and recycling all affect that ratio.
- The customs series uses HS02 through 2016 and HS17 from 2017 through 2024.
  HS 280461 covers electronic and solar polysilicon. HS 854140 in those
  versions covers cells and modules along with LEDs and other photosensitive
  devices. Price and shipment origin cannot identify individual solar stages.
- The throughput model explores assumptions about compatibility and timing.
  Its ranges make no claim about expected losses or the performance of a
  specific company or project.
