# Which parts of Europe's grid come from China, on a supply basis? — design draft (not yet filed)

Draft, 2026-09-21. Not a filing: the Prodcom and trade codes marked [VERIFY] are being confirmed, and
the design goes to two referees before it is filed. No value below has been read.

## Why

The transformer note found that China supplied 34% of the EU's transformer imports in 2024 but 9% of
its supply, and 52% of its imports of grain-oriented electrical steel but 24% of supply. Import shares
overstate dependence, sometimes by a factor of four. The grid is more than transformers; this applies
the same measure to the rest of it.

## The measure

As in the note's amendment E: apparent supply = EU27 sold production + imports from outside the EU
− exports to outside the EU, by value, 2019-2024 (Prodcom has no 2025 yet); China's share of imports
and of apparent supply, side by side, for each component and year. The reading rule is the note's:
where the supply share is under half the import share, the import share is not read as dependence.

## What this cannot say

Sold production excludes what a maker uses itself; production is at factory gate and imports include
freight; Prodcom suppresses confidential cells, reported as missing, never as zero; the Prodcom-to-HS
matches rest on product descriptions; apparent supply is not consumption. Value only.
