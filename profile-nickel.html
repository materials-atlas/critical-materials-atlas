The origin gap. Nickel, unwrought is exported mainly by 🇳🇴 Norway (18% of world trade) but mined mainly in 🇮🇩 Indonesia (50%), while Norway mines almost none of it. Taken at face value, import-origin statistics point to Norway as the source; one layer upstream, the real dependence is on Indonesia. Gap: +18 points.
