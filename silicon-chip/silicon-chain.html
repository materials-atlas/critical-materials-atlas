Semiconductor value chain

How concentration and control change from silicon to chips

China leads silicon-metal production. Germany and the United States lead in electronic-grade polysilicon, Japanese firms are among the largest blank-wafer suppliers, and the geography of chipmaking depends on the type of chip.

Evidence review · 24 November 2026 · Claim and source register

Where are production and supplier control concentrated, and how easily could buyers replace a supplier? The evidence compares countries, companies and customs categories to answer the first question. The second also requires evidence on qualified alternatives, spare capacity and replacement times.

Fabrication capacity shifted toward East Asia

The combined share of the United States and Europe fell from 81% in 1990 to 18% in 2022, a drop of 63 percentage points. Japan, Korea, Taiwan and China together rose from 17% to 76%, an increase of 59 percentage points. Atlas calculations from SIA/BCG (2024), Exhibit 7.

The table uses million 8-inch-equivalent wafer starts per month, a capacity measure by fab location. Actual output depends on utilization and yield. A fab can cover several chip types, so the categories overlap. OECD defines advanced logic as below 20 nm and mature logic as 20 nm and above. OECD (2025), Section 3, Figures 1 and 4.

The blank-wafer market through the cycle

SEMI reports worldwide shipment area and nominal revenue for silicon wafers used in semiconductors. In 2025, area shipped rose 5.8% to 12,973 million square inches while nominal revenue fell 1.2% to $11.4 billion. The chart shows rounded revenue from annual releases. SEMI’s 2025 year-end release supplies the published growth rates.

SEMI excludes solar applications. HS 381800 covers a broader set of products, including doped materials besides silicon, and measures customs trade.

Electronic-grade polysilicon

Demand and production. Electronic-grade polysilicon requires at least 11N purity. IEA-PVPS (2024), p. 50 reports 38,800 tonnes produced for semiconductors in 2023. SIA (2025), pp. 3–5 and Figure 1 forecast 33,500 tonnes of semiconductor demand and 1,379,400 tonnes of solar demand for 2025. Semiconductor demand was about 2.4% of that forecast total by weight.
Country and company estimates. OECD (2026), Chapter 2 reports about 65% of the global semiconductor-grade market for Germany and the United States combined and less than 10% for China, citing a 2025 industry report. SIA (2025), p. 4 estimates Wacker and Hemlock together at about three-quarters of the supplier market.

What the customs data cover

CEPII BACI records bilateral shipments for broad customs categories. HS 280469 gives silicon-metal trade context. HS 280461 combines solar- and semiconductor-grade polysilicon. HS 381800 also covers other doped elements and compounds. Export values describe what crosses borders and can include re-exports.

Why supplier qualification matters

Siltronic’s new 300 mm wafer plant in Singapore produced its first test wafers in late 2023. Early customer qualifications followed in 2024, and the company reported key customer qualifications in 2025. The milestones show that a plant can produce test wafers before it is ready to supply particular customers. Siltronic’s 2024 results presentation, slide 6 and 2025 annual report, p. 6.

These dates describe the ramp-up of one new plant. Estimating how quickly buyers could replace interrupted supply would require customer-specific approvals, available capacity, inventories and delivery dates.
