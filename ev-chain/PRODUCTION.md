## EU trade measures added · 25 November 2026

The page had no mention of EU trade measures. A section now gives the dates, scope and rates of the countervailing
duties on battery-electric cars from China (Regulations (EU) 2024/1866 and 2024/2754) and of the provisional safeguard
on hybrid cars (Regulation (EU) 2026/2614, in force 1 December 2026), each read in the legal text on 25 November 2026.
It quotes the Commission's own statement linking the duties to the rise in hybrid imports (2026/2614, recital 25) and
its import counts (tables 2 and 6). The page presents this as the Commission's provisional conclusion and makes no
causal estimate of its own. Two details in the texts differ: recital 23 of 2026/2614 dates the provisional duties to
4 July 2024, while Regulation 2024/1866 entered into force on 5 July 2024; the page uses the latter.
