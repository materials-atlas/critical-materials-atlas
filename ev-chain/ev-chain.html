Value chains · electric vehicles

Electric-vehicle supply depends on where cars and parts are made

China leads global electric-car assembly and has even larger shares of several battery production stages. Its share of EU imports fell for new battery-electric cars and rose for new petrol plug-in hybrids. This study measures those exposures separately and models how many imported vehicles would need to be replaced during a specified interruption.

Research briefing · revised 24 November 2026

EU import exposure moved in opposite directions

Findings addendum · 24 November 2026

How did China-origin EV import exposure change by powertrain, and what can the customs records say about replacement needs? From 2024 to 2025 China’s share of extra-EU imports of new battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) fell from 66.8% to 59.2%. Its share of extra-EU new petrol plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) rose from 21.3% to 45.3%. The denominators are total extra-EU units in each of the two selected product codes. Eurostat Comext.

Registrations give a wider comparison

These ratios compare gross customs arrivals with all new passenger-car registrations reported in the corresponding fuel category. The records track different events. Arrivals can be held in inventory, exported later or registered in another year. Registrations include EU-made cars and have no origin link to the customs data. Some national returns pool diesel plug-ins with petrol plug-ins or report plug-ins under other hybrid categories, so the petrol-PHEV comparison is approximate. Assembly and component production

How differently does supply exposure look when measured at the assembly plant, in EU imports or in the components fitted to a vehicle? In 2025, the International Energy Agency (IEA) estimated that China made around 16 million of the nearly 22 million electric cars produced worldwide, counting battery-electric and plug-in hybrid cars. About a quarter of global production was traded between major production and demand centres. The IEA estimated that China accounted for 40% of that global electric-car trade. The output and export shares have different denominators. IEA, Manufacturing and trade.

EU import exposure changed differently by powertrain

The EU recorded about the same number of China-origin new battery-electric cars (BEVs) in both years as imports from other countries grew. China-origin imports of new petrol plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) roughly quadrupled and made up a larger fraction of those imports. The customs-value share gives a second view of the same trade. For both vehicle classes it was below the unit share in 2025. Customs value reflects the mix of vehicles and declared values. Eurostat Comext.

What an import interruption would require buyers to replace

Modelled Take each 2025 calendar quarter in turn and cut China-origin imports of each new-car category by half. Hold all other baseline flows fixed. The gross gap is half the recorded China-origin arrivals in that quarter, rounded to the nearest vehicle with ties rounded up. It is the number of equivalent cars needed to restore baseline availability before crediting stocks or substitutes.

What would establish a binding production constraint?

A component is binding within a disruption window if getting more of it would restore feasible deliveries, with other constraints held fixed. The test needs plant and platform data on planned output, parts per vehicle, current inventories, contracted allocations, compatible suppliers and the earliest time an alternative can ship. Track assembly loss and finished-vehicle import loss separately so a vehicle missing for both reasons is counted once. Compare the model with a documented disruption before reporting effects on real output or claiming that a given policy reduces them.

Revision note · 24 November 2026

The previous overview used a single battery-value percentage without a matched cost or price denominator, and treated concentrated upstream stages as binding constraints. This revision uses separate output and import measures and documents the limits on replacing a part or a finished vehicle.
